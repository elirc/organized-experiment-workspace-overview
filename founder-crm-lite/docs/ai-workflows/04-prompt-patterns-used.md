# Prompt Patterns Used

## Architecture prompt pattern

Bad:

"Build me a CRM."

Better:

"Design the initial structure for a realistic CRM with roles, leads, deals, tasks, timelines, and import/export."

Strong:

"I am building a founder CRM as one of two separate projects in the same workspace. Compare structure options, recommend one, document tradeoffs, and optimize for future reviewability and AI-assisted development."
